MECE Feature Scoring Methodology (Level Playing Field Rubric)
Mutually Exclusive & Collectively Exhaustive (MECE) sub-parameters ensure apples-to-apples comparison between luxury European, legacy Japanese, and Chinese EV models.
1. Comfort & Cabin
20%

Cabin space & ergonomics, ride suspension tuning, climate control zones, seating materials & NVH insulation.

2. Performance & Drivetrain
18%

Horsepower / instant torque delivery, 0–100 km/h responsiveness, AWD/4x4 capability & chassis dynamics.

3. Safety & ADAS
20%

NCAP safety rating, airbag count, Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB), Lane-Keep Assist & 360° camera suite.

4. In-Car Tech & OTA
16%

Central infotainment display, digital cluster, wireless CarPlay/Android Auto, mobile app telematics & OTA updates.

5. Efficiency & Range
14%

WLTP EV range / hybrid fuel economy (km/L), running cost per km in Sri Lanka & DC fast-charging / regen capability.

6. After-Sales & Resale Moat
12%

Islandwide authorized workshop footprint, spare-parts availability, manufacturer warranty & local resale confidence.

S · Sense: Strategy Palette & Disruption
Diagnosing Sri Lanka's post-ban competitive terrain.

Environment: Adaptive / Shaping

Low predictability (tariff & FX shifts) paired with high malleability (60% EV luxury tax edge & rapid Chinese OEM entry). Classical 5-year plans age in months; winners vary, select, and scale fast.

The Innovator's Dilemma in Action

Incumbents defend high-margin ICE heritage while disruptors (BYD, Jetour, Jaecoo, Kaiyi) enter lower with superior cabin tech and climb straight into the mainstream.

C · Choose: Blue Ocean Strategy Canvas
Traditional ICE Distributors vs. Chinese EV Entrants (1 = low, 5 = high).
C · Choose: ERRC Action Grid
Eliminate · Reduce · Raise · Create framework applied to Sri Lanka's EV transition.

Eliminate

Combustion engine complexity, high fuel dependency, and 100%–120% petrol/diesel luxury tax penalties.

Reduce

Landed price via vertical scale + 60% EV luxury tax tier; model refresh cycles cut to ~18 months vs. 48+ months.

Raise

ADAS driver-assist, large infotainment displays, and connected features delivered as standard in the LKR 10M–22M segment.

Create

Software-defined vehicles (OTA updates), bundled home-charging, and battery-health resale warranties.
